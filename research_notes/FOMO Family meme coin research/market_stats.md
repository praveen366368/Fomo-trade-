Research note for the "$1 into every promising new coin, take profit at X%" question. Compiled 2026-11-19.

**How to read this note (method and limits):**
- Every number carries its own date and, where known, its method. "Verified" here means the figure is stated by the primary publisher (study author, on-chain analytics firm, protocol) as relayed in search results; "claim" means a secondary/vendor/blog figure or one I could not tie to a primary page.

## 1. Launch volume: how many tokens launch per day, and where (2025-2026)

### Takeaway
Solana still produces roughly 25,000-40,000 new tokens a day, and in late 2026 pump.fun is again close to a monopoly (about 78% of all new Solana tokens from Nov 2025 to Oct 2026, rising to about 90% by Aug 2026, and 92% of launchpad launches across all chains in the 30 days to 9 Nov 2026). The 2025 challengers (LetsBonk, Believe, Boop, Moonshot, Zora on Base, four.meme on BNB) each had a burst of share and then faded.

## 2. Graduation rates, early death, rugs and pump-and-dumps

### Takeaway
Only about 0.2% to 2% of pump.fun launches graduate, depending on window and method: 1 in 58 (1.7%) over Nov 2025 to Oct 2026, but as low as 0.2% to 0.26% in May-June 2026. Most coins stop trading on launch day: 68.7% have their last curve trade on day 0 (CoinGecko), and 79% never trade after launch day (Bitquery). By liquidity-based definitions, about 98.6% end as rugs or pump-and-dumps (Solidus Labs).

### Cited Findings
**Graduation**
- Bitquery (1 Nov 2025 to 7 Oct 2026): **1 in 58 pump.fun coins graduated (~1.7%)**. A monthly series in the same coverage shows **~2% to 3.5% of launches graduating each month from Aug to mid-Oct 2026**. — [Bitquery](https://bitquery.io/investigations/solana-launchpad-year)
- Dune-based reporting: the **7-day average graduation rate fell to 0.26% in the week of 16 Jun 2026**, about **80% lower than three months earlier**. A Feb 2026 high of about **1.15%** was also reported. Solana daily fees fell to about **5,300 SOL/day in June 2026** from more than **33,000 SOL/day in January 2026**. — [MoneyCheck](https://moneycheck.com/pumpfun-graduation-rate-crashes-80-as-solana-fees-sink-to-5300-sol-in-june/); [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/pump-fun-activity-drops-solana-fees-decline/)
- Academic (Kamat, arXiv 2607.02823, July 2026, corrected v3): **832,941 launches observed 8 May to 10 Jun 2026**; **1,651 graduated** to PumpSwap within the observation window, a pooled rate of **0.198% (Wilson 95% CI 0.189-0.208%)**. Median **time-to-graduation 1.66 min** (p90 2.80 min, max 5.98 min). Launches advertising a Telegram channel had an **8.94x** higher graduation rate; Cox concordance 0.858. **Caveat from the authors:** the window was effectively about 6 minutes per token, so 0.198% is a lower bound, not the true 24-hour rate. They also withdrew a late-graduation recovery analysis (0 of 100 sampled mints showed graduation). The same paper cites a **0.63% graduation rate for Sept-Oct 2025** (655,770 tokens). — [arXiv 2607.02823v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02823v3); [Zenodo dataset RED-PUMP-2026-v1](https://zenodo.org/records/21383616)
- Practitioner cohort: on **13 Aug 2026**, **1,038 graduates**, **median launch-to-graduation about 2 minutes**, **2.7% graduation rate** (Medium/Coinmonks, not peer reviewed). — [Medium/Coinmonks](https://medium.com/coinmonks/pump-fun-api-how-to-track-bonding-curves-graduations-and-pumpswap-on-chain-879b689fbedb)
- Long-run: about **1.4% graduation** of ~1.8M tokens (Dune/Hashed-era analysis, 2024), and The Block's tracker showed **fewer than 2% of all pump.fun tokens ever graduated**. The Block publishes a daily "% graduated" series. — [Gate news](https://www.gate.com/news/detail/6706529); [The Block data](https://www.theblock.co/data/decentralized-finance/launchpads/pump-fun-percent-graduated-tokens-daily)
- Mechanics: since **20 Mar 2025** graduates migrate to **PumpSwap** (before that, Raydium). LP tokens are burned at graduation, so the migrated liquidity cannot be pulled. — [Blofin](https://blofin.com/en/academy/education/pumpfun/what-is-pumpswap); [ripr.trade](https://ripr.trade/blog/pump-fun-graduation-and-migration-explained)
- The Block reported that pump.fun's **graduation rate jumped after "BOOST" changed launch incentives** (2026). This is consistent with the higher Aug-Oct 2026 monthly rates above. — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/409815/robinhood-chain-deposits-climb-even-as-volume-and-daily-users-fade); [DEXTools, "Graduation Rate Collapses to 0.26%"](https://www.dextools.io/news/pump-fun-graduation-collapse-solana-fees-2026)
- **Mayhem Mode** (launched Nov 2025, opt-in at creation): it mints an **extra 1B tokens (total supply 2B)** for a pump.fun AI trading agent. The agent may trade the coin for its **first 24 hours**, pays **no protocol fees**, and burns whatever it has not sold. Because the agent can sell supply that no buyer paid into the curve, a Mayhem coin's price can fall **below** the normal launch-price floor (confirmed in my sample, Key Question 6). — [pump.fun docs](https://pump.fun/docs/mayhem-mode); [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/pump-fun-launches-mayhem-mode-with-ai-agents-to-boost-meme-token-trading); [Blofin, supply risk](https://blofin.com/academy/education/pumpfun/mayhem-mode-supply-risk); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/379285/pump-funs-new-mayhem-mode-fails-boost-token-launches-revenue)
- 2026 "BOOST" mode: about **20% of the liquidity** that used to be locked in the PumpSwap pool at graduation now funds automatic market buys in the **first five minutes after migration**, and the bought tokens are burned. — [KuCoin blog](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/pump-fun-boost-mode-explained)

**Death / survival**
- CoinGecko (pump.fun, 14 Jan 2024 to 18 Jun 2026; 18.67M tokens; method: the date of each token's last pump.fun bonding-curve trade): **68.67% (12.8M) had their last trade on launch day**; **80.37%** were dead by the day after launch; **only 4.55% (~850,180) were still trading after 90 days**. Caveat: curve trades only, so tokens that migrated to PumpSwap/Raydium/Meteora may be under-counted as "dead". CoinGecko does not label them scams. — [KuCoin/CoinGecko](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/coingecko-study-only-4-55-of-pump-fun-tokens-survive-90-days); [CryptoPotato](https://cryptopotato.com/nearly-70-of-pump-fun-tokens-die-on-launch-day-coingecko/); [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/pump-funs-token-factory-has-a-69-launch-day-death-rate-coingecko/)
- Bitquery (1 Nov 2025 to 7 Oct 2026): **79% of 8,984,141 pump.fun tokens never traded after launch day**. Bitquery stresses that "die" means no trades, not zero price. — [Bitquery](https://bitquery.io/investigations/solana-launchpad-year)
- Pump.fun's own revenue mix (30 days to 9 Nov 2026): about **75% of fees come from tokens under 1 hour old**, so trading activity is concentrated in the first hour. — [TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/investing/54556)

**Rugs and pump-and-dumps**
- Solidus Labs (report May 2025; pump.fun tokens Jan 2024 to Mar 2025): **98.6%** of pump.fun tokens, out of more than 7 million, "collapsed into pump-and-dump schemes" (98.7% in another passage). Definition: the token's liquidity fell **below $1,000**. Only **~97,000** kept at least $1,000 of liquidity. About **93% of 388,000 Raydium pools (361,000)** showed "soft rug pull" characteristics. Largest single rug: **$1.9M (MToken)**. Pump.fun disputed the report ("lacks a basic understanding of memecoins"). — [Solidus Labs](https://www.soliduslabs.com/reports/solana-rug-pulls-pump-dumps-crypto-compliance); [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2025/05/07/98-of-tokens-on-pump-fun-have-been-rug-pulls-or-an-act-of-fraud-new-report-says)
- Chainalysis 2025 Crypto Crime Report (calendar 2024, all chains): **2,063,519 tokens launched**, **873,957 listed on DEXs**, **74,037 (3.59%) met suspected pump-and-dump criteria**. About **89-90%** of those pools were rugged by the same address that created the pool. A broader "may be linked" share of **4.52%** is also reported. These are pattern-based flags, not court findings. — [Tekedia](https://www.tekedia.com/3-59-of-tokens-launched-in-2024-showed-signs-of-pump-and-dump-schemes-chainalysis-report-reveals/); [CryptoRank](https://cryptorank.io/news/feed/6a65e-89-of-dex-pools-exploited-by-creators-in-pump-and-dump-schemes-chainalysis); [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/chainalysis-over-4-5-of-all-tokens-launched-in-2024-have-pump-and-dump-traits/)
- Chainalysis 2026 Crypto Crime Report (covers 2025): headline **$154B illicit volume**. No memecoin-specific pump-and-dump percentage was found in search results. — [Chainalysis on X](https://x.com/chainalysis/status/2029539952128622847)
- Academic: "Catching the Rug" (arXiv 2608.20271, Aug 2026) builds a dataset of **6.4 million Solana memecoins over 7 months**. It compares a liquidity-withdrawal (TVL) rug definition with an inactivity ("idle") definition, with the idle-based rug assessed at **1 hour**. It finds a significant distribution shift between pump.fun and Raydium tokens. — [arXiv 2608.20271](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.20271v1)
- Academic: "Meme Coin Factories" (Szwajcok et al., arXiv 2609.10246, CCS 2026) collects all **~15 million pump.fun coins over two years** and identifies five manipulation classes: wash trading, creator-address obfuscation, coordinated selling, copycat coins, and social-media manipulation. It also documents "Market-Manipulation-as-a-Service" tools and finds some strategies effective at raising prices and extracting value. — [arXiv 2609.10246](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10246)

### Inferences
- "Graduation rate" figures differ by up to 10x mainly because of the measurement window. A 6-minute window gives about 0.2%; a 7-day average about 0.26-1.15%; Bitquery's whole-period share about 1.7%; monthly windows in Aug-Oct 2026 about 2-3.5%. For a strategy, the useful number is "share of the coins I buy that ever graduate", which is probably about 1-3% in late 2026 for an unfiltered feed.
- "Dead" in the CoinGecko and Bitquery studies means **no further trades**. On a *standard* (1B-supply) pump.fun bonding curve, price cannot fall below the launch price, about $2.07K FDV at SOL $73.90, and a dead coin can still be sold back to the curve. So "dead" does not mean −100% for a buyer near the launch price. The maximum loss on the curve is about 1 − (launch price / entry price): for example −52% for an entry at $4.3K FDV and −90% only for entries above about $20.7K FDV. The −100% outcomes come **after graduation** (an AMM pool drained by sellers) or in **Mayhem Mode (2B-supply) coins**, where the agent's extra supply can push the price below the launch level. Solidus' "<$1,000 liquidity" definition measures the same abandonment, not necessarily theft.

### Gaps
- No primary 2025-2026 study gave a clean "% of tokens at ~zero within 1 hour / within 24 hours" in price terms. CoinGecko and Bitquery measure trading cessation by day. My own sample (Key Question 6) gives a 1-hour price-based view for one afternoon.
- No 2026 Solidus Labs update was found, and Chainalysis' 2026 memecoin chapter figures were not found.

## 3. Return distribution: how often coins do 2x/10x/100x/1000x, how fast, at what market caps, and what happens after the peak

### Takeaway
No study found reports launch-price multiples directly. Pump.fun's curve math links multiples to market caps: launch is about $2.1K FDV at SOL $73.90, graduation is about 14.7x launch (about $30K FDV), 100x is about $207K, and 1000x is about $2.07M. On that mapping, the base rates are graduation 0.2-2%, about 1 in 78,000 coins above $100K market cap (9 Nov 2026), and about 1 in 20,000 or fewer ever above $1M. The winners move within minutes (median time to graduation about 2 minutes), and graduates usually dump right after migration (38% touched −80% within 10 minutes in an Oct 2026 sample).

### Cited Findings
- As of **9 Nov 2026**, **312 of ~24.4M cumulative pump.fun launches had market caps above $100,000**, about **1 in 78,000**. — [TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/investing/54556)
- 2024-era $1M snapshots: Decrypt counted **89 pump.fun coins above $1M** (~0.0045% of ~2M tokens). Another analysis found **41 of 1.7M** had held **>$1M for several weeks (~0.002%)**. A Dune/Hashed analysis put the odds of exceeding $1M at **~1 in 20,000** and graduation at **1.4%** of ~1.8M. About **0.3% of graduated tokens** had a market cap above $1M (same search set). — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/249107/fewer-than-100-pump-fun-tokens-above-1m-market-cap-amid-meme-coin-lull); [Protos](https://protos.com/research-finds-less-than-0-002-of-pump-fun-memecoins-succeed/); [Gate news](https://www.gate.com/news/detail/6706529)
- **Speed:** median launch-to-graduation was **1.66 min** (p90 2.80 min) for May-June 2026 graduates (arXiv 2607.02823) and about **2 min** for a 13 Aug 2026 cohort (Medium). Coins that make the ~15x trip to graduation therefore mostly do it within about 3 minutes of launch. — [arXiv 2607.02823v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02823v3); [Medium/Coinmonks](https://medium.com/coinmonks/pump-fun-api-how-to-track-bonding-curves-graduations-and-pumpswap-on-chain-879b689fbedb)
- **Right after graduation** (practitioner study, 2,193 graduates, 10-12 Oct 2026): **38.1% (835) touched −80%** within the first 10 minutes. The median coin's 10-minute low was about **half** its price 10 seconds after graduation. Large single-sale dumps came at a **median 2.2 minutes** after graduation. The author found the "fast-filling curve = bundled rug" filter did not hold up. — [DEV Community / launchtape](https://dev.to/launchtape/what-2193-pumpfun-graduations-did-in-their-first-10-minutes-and-what-it-means-for-your-bot-4f70)
- **24-48h after graduation** (Coinmonks snapshot, 18 Nov 2026): only **19.6% of 759** graduates aged 24-48h had at least one trade in the past hour; the top 10% of coins took **75.5%** of 24h volume. — [Medium/Coinmonks, "What Happens After a Pump.fun Coin Graduates? We Tracked 1,634 of Them"](https://medium.com/coinmonks/what-happens-after-a-pump-fun-coin-graduates-we-tracked-1-634-of-them-61efab687f1b)
- **Incentive to dump before migration:** Marino, Naviglio, Tarantelli & Lillo (arXiv 2602.14860, Feb 2026) model the probability of graduation from SOL locked in the curve plus launch-structure and trader-behaviour variables. As summarized by a secondary source, the paper finds that selling just before graduation yields more SOL than selling right after, which gives large early holders a reason to exit before migration. — [arXiv 2602.14860](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14860); [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6543715); [Emergent Mind summary](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/pump-fun)
- Survivorship: **4.55%** of pump.fun tokens still traded after 90 days (CoinGecko, launches to 18 Jun 2026). — [KuCoin/CoinGecko](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/coingecko-study-only-4-55-of-pump-fun-tokens-survive-90-days)
- Team-following: Gafarov (arXiv 2611.11436, Nov 2026) reconstructs 10 pump.fun windows (Apr 2024 to Sept 2026). Following hindsight-ranked creator "teams" (funding-linked wallet groups) gives large retrospective returns, but **only 3 of 40 tests reject the null after Holm adjustment** in selection-matched nulls. The author stresses that the selection is retrospective and not a causal test. — [arXiv 2611.11436](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.11436)

### Inferences
- **Curve-math mapping (my calculation, standard pump.fun parameters):** 30 virtual SOL and 1.073B virtual tokens give a launch market cap of about 27.96 SOL, about **$2,066 at SOL $73.90**. My live pull matched this: fresh pump.fun pools showed FDV $2,064-2,073. Graduation at about 85 real SOL gives about 411 SOL FDV, about **$30.4K**, which is **~14.7x the launch price**. So:
  - 2x is about $4.1K FDV.
  - 10x is about $20.7K FDV, still on the curve.
  - Graduation is about 14.7x.
  - 100x is about $207K FDV.
  - 1000x is about $2.07M FDV.
- **Implied base rates for a buyer at the launch price:** about 1-3% of coins ever see roughly 15x (graduation). Fewer than about 0.01-0.05% ever see 100x (the $100K-$1M range; only 312 of 24.4M were above $100K on 9 Nov 2026, though more touched it and fell). About 0.005% or fewer ever see 1000x (above $2M). A buyer entering a few minutes after launch has already paid 1.5x-10x the launch price, so the same coins offer proportionally smaller multiples.
- 2x moves are common on the curve because small buys move price a lot near launch. The time window is very short, though: the median graduate does its whole move in about 2 minutes, and post-graduation dumps come at about 2 minutes. A take-profit strategy is therefore mostly a latency and fill-quality problem, not a stock-picking problem.

### Gaps
- No study was found giving the full distribution of maximum multiples from launch (P(max ≥ 2x/10x/100x/1000x)) for 2025-2026 cohorts. No study was found giving time-to-peak or typical drawdown-from-peak for the whole population, or a measured expected value of buying at or after graduation. The figures above are proxies.
- A Raydium-era graduate study (15,548 graduates: 43.8% kept $5K liquidity at 30 min, 19.7% at 24h; median 24h liquidity ~$2,888) surfaced in search without a confirmable source page, so it is not used.

## 4. Who wins: devs/bundlers, snipers, insiders, bots vs retail; wallet profitability; MEV and slippage

### Takeaway
Creator ("dev") wallets and their bundled wallets capture the largest identified profits, at least $579M after fees through 30 Jun 2026, against about $31.5M for snipers. Most buyers lose: 74% of 2.9M Solana memecoin buyers held less SOL after two weeks (median −$13), and about 90% of FOMO-app wallets lost money after fees in 2026. CoinGecko's realized-only metric showed 73% of pump.fun wallets "profitable" in April 2026, which is not comparable. Sandwich bots took about $29.7M gross over 11 months, 96% of it on pump.fun venues. Trading apps took about $422M in fees over 10 months, a cost that falls hardest on small tickets.

### Cited Findings
**Insiders, bundlers, snipers**
- Bitquery: on bundled launches through **30 Jun [2026]**, devs and their bundled wallets made **at least $579M after pump.fun fees** (on the portion sold). Sniper bots made **$31.5M after fees** over the same period. **38% of new pump.fun coins had a non-dev wallet buying in block 0.** (The publication year is inferred from Bitquery's 2025-2026 series and should be checked.) — [Bitquery, "Pump.fun Devs' Bundles Made $579M+, Snipers Just $31.5M"](https://bitquery.io/investigations/pump-fun-snipers-bundles)
- Pine Analytics (2025): more than **15,000 SOL** of realized profit in one month from dev-linked sniping across more than **15,000 launches**, with **87%** of those sniper trades profitable. The authors call this a floor, because snipers using proxies or burner wallets were missed. — [PANews](https://panews.io/articles/416583xr); [Gate news](https://www.gate.com/news/detail/10385858)
- arXiv 2607.02795 (Jul 2026) detects **1,012 persistent coordinated sniper wallet rings** on pump.fun and estimates their first-hour buyer-flow lift. Detail beyond the title was not obtained. — [arXiv 2607.02795](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.02795)
- A bundle-checker comparison shows the same mint read as **8% bundled by one tool and 103% by another**, so bundle-share statistics depend heavily on method. — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/julio_627b13a09cd9ff90122/why-two-solana-bundle-checkers-give-you-two-different-numbers-2ph5)

**Wallet profitability (different methods give very different answers)**
- Bitquery (published ~Oct 2026): **2.9M Solana memecoin buyers** followed through **10 launch weeks** (Nov 2025 to Aug 2026). FOMO's fees were **~$40M (≈0.5% of trading dollars)**, about **10% of users' aggregate losses**; the rest was token losses. Aggregate loss is about $395M baseline, with a range of $204M (uncapped last prices) to $490M (unsold tokens valued at zero). Caveat: FOMO has not confirmed the signer/fee addresses, and token-denominated fees are excluded. — [Bitquery](https://bitquery.io/investigations/fomo-app-traders-lose-money); [TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/investing/43767)

Only **5.4%** made more than $1,000 in April 2026, and 65.1% of all wallets made $1-$500. Because only realized trades count, unsold losing bags are excluded, which flatters the result. A Dune dashboard (@oladee) found **49-50.6% of wallets lost money in March 2026**. — [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/pump-fun-traders-are-making-a-comeback); [Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2026/05/277755-solana-based-meme-coins-trading-platform-pump-fun-makes-recovery-research/)

About **30%** of wallets had made only one sell order, suggesting bots. **Trading-app fees (a transfer from small traders)**
- Bitquery: **25 Solana trading apps took $422.4M in fees over 711 million swaps** from 30 Oct 2025 to 30 Aug 2026; **Axiom took 48%**. Including Jito tips, traders paid about **$438M** (Axiom 46%). App-routed trades paid **12% of all Jito tips**. — [Bitquery](https://bitquery.io/investigations/solana-jito-tips-trading-app-fees)

**MEV / sandwiches**
- Bitquery (11 months to ~2026): about **6.85-6.9M sandwich attacks** took **$29.7M gross (before bot costs)** from memecoin buyers; **96% were on pump.fun venues** (curve + PumpSwap). Victims' loss was **1.3x the bot take on PumpSwap and 1.2x on the launch curve**. The average take was about **$4.34 per sandwich**. In a 63-attack cost sample, bots kept **68%** of gross and **18 attacks lost money**. Other bots' trades made up 39% of the estimated gross take. The paper notes validators rebuilt private mempools after Jito shut its public mempool in **Mar 2024**. sandwiched.me presenters (Accelerate 2025) estimated **$370-500M extracted over 16 months**. Jito's Oct 2025 validator bans referenced attacks aimed mostly at specific apps and **small meme-token trades** (about 222,272 victims). — [IMC'26 paper](https://cnitarot.github.io/papers/imc26_solana.pdf); [Solana Compass, Accelerate 2025](https://solanacompass.com/learn/accelerate-25/scale-or-die-at-accelerate-2025-the-state-of-solana-mev); [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/lightspeed-newsletter-solana-sandwich-attacks); [Helius MEV report](https://www.helius.dev/blog/solana-mev-report)

### Inferences
- Bitquery's fee totals imply an **average app fee of about $0.59 per swap** ($422.4M / 711M swaps, my arithmetic). Large trades skew that mean, but it shows why a $1 ticket cannot carry a percentage app fee plus a fixed priority-fee and tip bundle.
- A sandwich bot's take from one victim is capped by the victim's slippage tolerance times trade size. A **$1 buy at 10% slippage exposes at most about $0.10**, which is below the bot's own costs; on the pump.fun curve the bot also pays about 1.25% per leg on its larger notional. So **$1 tickets are economically unattractive to sandwich**, and the sandwich risk to this strategy is small. The real costs at $1 are fixed fees and adverse selection (buying from snipers and devs who are exiting).
- The profit-sharing picture is consistent across sources. Creators and bundlers, who start with free or near-free supply, and fast bots take the profits. The median buyer loses a small amount, and Bitquery found that diversifying across many coins did not fix this. Diversification does not help because the expected value per coin is negative after fees, not merely noisy.

### Gaps
- No study was found that splits the profits from *winning* tokens specifically between snipers, bundlers, devs and retail. Bitquery's $579M / $31.5M covers all bundled launches.
- No 2026 measurement was found of typical realized slippage on $1-$10 trades in low-liquidity pools; price-impact math is in Key Question 5.
- One claim ("top 250 token issuers earned ~$79M", Mar 2026) surfaced without a confirmable source and is left out.

## 5. Cost of a $1 round trip (Solana, Nov 2026)

### Takeaway
At SOL $73.90, a lean self-built bot trading pump.fun's curve pays about **3.3%** round trip on a $1 position, plus **$0.15 (15%)** of refundable token-account rent tied up. With typical trading-app defaults (0.001 SOL priority fee + 0.001 SOL tip per transaction, plus about 1% app fee per side), the round trip is about **34% of a $1 position**, which wipes out any take-profit below +35%. Fixed per-transaction costs, not percentage fees, decide whether a $1 ticket can work.

### Cited Findings
- **Base fee:** 5,000 lamports per signature (half burned); priority fee optional and paid to the validator. — [Bitquery](https://bitquery.io/investigations/solana-jito-tips-trading-app-fees)
- **Priority fees in 2026 (secondary/vendor measurements):** average priority fee about **0.0000514 SOL**, with about 71% of user transactions paying one. A 5 Aug 2026 measurement found "medium ≈ free, high ≈ 0.00005 SOL, above 0.01 SOL is waste". One bot guide quotes a "standard" **0.001-0.005 SOL** per trade. — [devrels.xyz](https://devrels.xyz/articles/solana-priority-fees-2026); [solbotrank](https://solbotrank.com/guides/solana-priority-fees-and-slippage); [Raiden terminal blog](https://terminal.raiden.wtf/blog/solana-priority-fee-tip-right-now)
- **Jito tips:** minimum **1,000 lamports**, median about **4,000 lamports** in an Aug 2026 sample, and p75 suggested for non-MEV use. Jito tips are reportedly more than 60% of all priority-fee volume. — [RPC Fast](https://rpcfast.com/blog/jito-explained-bundles-tips-mev-solana); [devrels.xyz](https://devrels.xyz/articles/solana-priority-fees-2026)
- **Trading-terminal defaults:** Axiom's documented defaults are **0.001 SOL priority + 0.001 SOL bribe (tip)**; spot fee about **1%** (0.95% for new accounts, 0.75% at top tier) (third-party guides). — [Axiompedia](https://axiompedia.com/guides/trading/best-axiom-trade-settings); [Coin Bureau](https://coinbureau.com/review/axiom-trade-review)
- **FOMO app fee:** ≈**0.5%** of trading dollars (Bitquery's estimate from fee-account flows, Jan-Oct 2026). — [Bitquery](https://bitquery.io/investigations/fomo-app-traders-lose-money)
- **Pump.fun protocol fees (secondary sources; pump.fun's own fee page was not reachable):** bonding curve **1.25% per trade** (≈0.95% protocol + 0.30% creator; fee schedule last updated 20 May 2026). PumpSwap fees are tiered by market cap, **from 1.25% at low caps down to 0.30% at $20M+**. Under Project Ascend (Sept 2025), the creator fee is **0.95% for $88K-$300K market caps**, falling to 0.05% at $20M. The creator pays a one-time **0.015 SOL** graduation fee. A **0.45% (0.40% with referral) "platform fee"** on trades placed in pump.fun's own apps since Oct 2026 is reported by one source only (unconfirmed). Older guides citing 1% curve / 0.25% PumpSwap are outdated. — [Froglabs](https://froglabs.io/blog/pump-fun-fees-explained); [soltokencreator](https://www.soltokencreator.io/blog/pump-fun-fees-explained); [Token Metrics](https://tokenmetrics.com/blog/pump-fun-social-trading-review/); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/337872/pump-funs-new-fee-model-hands-out-2m-to-creators-in-first-24-hours); [DEXTools](https://www.dextools.io/tutorials/what-is-pump-fun-project-ascend-and-the-new-creator-fees)
- **Token account rent:** the standard SPL token account (165 bytes) needs **2,039,280 lamports (0.00203928 SOL)** to be rent-exempt, refundable when the account is closed. This is standard Solana protocol knowledge; the search summary flagged it as not re-verified against current docs. — [solbotrank](https://solbotrank.com/guides/solana-priority-fees-and-slippage)

### Inferences
My arithmetic, at SOL = $73.90 (2026-11-19):

| Item | SOL | USD | % of $1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base fee, 1 signature | 0.000005 | $0.00037 | 0.04% per tx |
| "High" priority fee (Aug 2026 measurement) | 0.00005 | $0.0037 | 0.37% per tx |
| Jito tip, median ~4,000 lamports | 0.000004 | $0.0003 | 0.03% per tx |
| App-default priority + tip (0.001 + 0.001) | 0.002 | $0.148 | 14.8% per tx |
| Token account rent (refundable on close) | 0.00203928 | $0.151 | 15.1% locked |
| Pump.fun curve fee | — | — | 1.25% per side |
| App/router fee | — | — | ~0.5% (FOMO) to ~1% (Axiom) per side |
| Price impact of $1 (0.0135 SOL) on a fresh curve (30 virtual SOL) | — | — | ≈0.045% (negligible) |

- **Scenario A, lean self-run bot, curve, no app fee:** 2 × 1.25% + 2 × (0.000055 SOL = $0.0041) ≈ **3.3% round trip**. Closing the token account in the sell transaction recovers the $0.15 rent. If the account is never closed (for example the coin is abandoned and the dust is never burned), the rent is lost and the loss rises by about **15%**.
- **Scenario B, typical app/terminal defaults:** 2 × (1.25% + 1%) + 2 × $0.148 ≈ **34% round trip**. A +50% take-profit nets only about +12% after costs, and a +100% take-profit about +61%. The same settings on a $10 ticket cost about 7.5%, and on a $100 ticket about 4.8%.
- **Scenario C, FOMO-style app (≈0.5% fee):** about 2 × (1.25% + 0.5%) = 3.5% plus whatever network fee and tip the app sets. FOMO's network-fee handling was not found, so this scenario is incomplete.
- Price impact is negligible at $1. Slippage risk at this size comes from **latency**: by the time a signal fires and the buy lands, the median graduate's whole move takes about 2 minutes. MEV is a minor risk at $1, as explained in Key Question 4.

### Gaps
- Pump.fun's official fee page (exact PumpSwap tier breakpoints, and whether the app platform fee applies to API/bot trades) could not be read.
- It was not confirmed whether pump.fun still requires an extra per-user PDA (for example a volume-accumulator account) on first buy, which would add another refundable rent deposit.
- No 2026 data was found on median priority fees during launch-time congestion; costs at the busiest launch moments are likely higher than the averages above.
