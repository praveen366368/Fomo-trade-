# Data, News and Execution APIs for an Automated Solana Meme-Coin Bot (state as of 19 Nov 2026)

Scope: catalogue of market-data, new-launch detection, token-safety, execution/landing and social/news services for a bot that buys about $1 of many new coins and sells at a take-profit. Live tests were run with curl from this container on 2026-11-19 at about 19:15–19:30 UTC. Each live result is cited to the exact URL tested and labelled "live test". SOL price at test time was about $73.66, from Jupiter Price v3. All other facts come from web search results (WebFetch was blocked for doc hosts outside the allowlist, so docs were reached through search summaries). Prices are dated where the source dates them.

## Market data APIs (Dexscreener, GeckoTerminal, Birdeye, GMGN, Moralis, Bitquery, CoinGecko, Codex, Mobula, SolanaTracker, Jupiter)

### Takeaway
A complete keyless REST stack works today. Dexscreener handles discovery and pair stats, Jupiter Price v3 and Tokens v2 handle price, liquidity, holder and audit stats, and GeckoTerminal is a backup. All three are REST-poll with 5–60 s CDN caching, so they suit take-profit monitoring of $1 positions but are too slow for first-block sniping. Paid tiers (Birdeye $99+/mo, SolanaTracker €397+/mo for websockets, Codex Growth, Bitquery $69–$239/mo) mainly add websockets, higher rate limits and historical data.

### Inferences
- The free stack needs no keys: Dexscreener (profiles, boosts, pairs, up to 300 rpm), Jupiter Tokens v2 `/recent` (new tokens with stats and audit), Price v3 (5 s cache) and GeckoTerminal new_pools (30 s cache, about 10–30 rpm). That covers discovery and take-profit polling for many $1 positions if requests are batched (multi-mint Dexscreener calls and multi-id Jupiter price calls).
- CDN caching of 5–60 s on every free endpoint means price checks lag by up to a minute. A take-profit on volatile pump tokens should be triggered from on-chain data (a websocket on the pool or bonding-curve account) or Jupiter Price v3 (5 s cache) rather than Dexscreener (30 s cache).
- Jupiter Tokens v2 already returns `audit.mintAuthorityDisabled`, `freezeAuthorityDisabled`, `topHoldersPercentage`, `devMints` and `organicScore`, so it doubles as a free first-pass safety filter.

### Gaps
- Dexscreener's official per-endpoint limits and maximum addresses per request (commonly cited as 30) could not be read directly because docs.dexscreener.com was blocked for WebFetch. No rate-limit headers are returned, so limits cannot be observed. I found no documented Dexscreener websocket in the public API.
- Exact current prices for Codex Growth, Mobula plans and Moralis tiers were not visible in the search results.
- Whether the GeckoTerminal 429 was caused by a shared container IP or by the 10 rpm keyless limit is unknown.

## Real-time new-launch detection (pump.fun, PumpPortal, Helius, Triton/Yellowstone, Shyft, QuickNode, Chainstack)

### Takeaway
The cheapest real-time path is PumpPortal's free `subscribeNewToken` and migration websocket. Next is a Helius plan: Developer at $49/mo includes Enhanced WebSockets from Apr 2026, and webhooks cost 1 credit per event. The fastest is a Yellowstone-compatible gRPC stream: Chainstack add-on from $49/mo, Shyft from $199/mo, SolanaTracker €200/mo, QuickNode included in Scale at $499/mo, Helius LaserStream on Business at $499/mo, or Triton at about $0.08/GB. pump.fun's own frontend API is undocumented and returned a Cloudflare challenge to this container.

### Inferences
- For a $1-per-coin bot, a reasonable budget path is to start with the PumpPortal free new-token websocket plus Jupiter Tokens v2 `/recent` polling, then upgrade to Chainstack's $49 gRPC or Helius Developer for direct on-chain subscriptions on the pump program. Sub-100 ms shred-level feeds (LaserStream, RabbitStream, raw shreds) only pay off when competing for the first blocks, and that tier ($199–$1,000+/mo) far exceeds the expected P&L of $1 trades.
- The public RPC's pubsub limit of 5 subscriptions makes it unsuitable as the production launch feed.
- New pump.fun mints are Token-2022, so parsers and ATA creation must handle that token program.

### Gaps
- PumpPortal's own docs and fees page could not be fetched (pumpportal.fun was blocked). Data-WS rate limits and connection caps are unverified.
- No independent latency benchmarks comparing PumpPortal WS, Helius Enhanced WS, LaserStream and Yellowstone providers were found.
- The QuickNode post's "June 1" effective date did not state a year in the snippet.

## Token safety APIs (RugCheck, SolSniffer, GoPlus, De.Fi, TokenSniffer, Birdeye security, Jupiter Shield)

### Takeaway
RugCheck (keyless, about 15 req per window) and Jupiter Shield plus Tokens v2 audit (keyless) together cover mint and freeze authority, LP lock, holder concentration, insiders, creator history and "not sellable" flags, at no cost. GoPlus Solana (beta, keyless per a third party) is a cross-check. Birdeye security needs a key. SolSniffer, TokenSniffer and De.Fi are less suited to Solana or lack published Solana API details.

### Cited Findings
- Live test: `GET https://api.rugcheck.xyz/v1/tokens/{mint}/report/summary` returned 200 keyless in 0.34 s with headers `x-rate-limit-limit: 15`, `x-rate-limit-remaining: 14`. Fields: `tokenProgram, tokenType, risks[{name, value, description, score, level}], score, score_normalised, lpLockedPct`. Example on a seconds-old pump token: risks "Creator history of rugged tokens" (score 120000, danger), "Single holder ownership 49.89%" (danger) and "High holder concentration" (warn), with `score_normalised` 81 and `lpLockedPct` 100. — [live test](https://api.rugcheck.xyz/v1/tokens/6Jhj71pZztUHh6j1Au6yA61Lqv1bpm11iFc7Lav4pump/report/summary)
- Live test: `GET https://api.rugcheck.xyz/v1/tokens/{mint}/report` returned 200 keyless with the full report. ## Execution path (Jupiter, Raydium, pump.fun/PumpSwap direct, PumpPortal, Jito, priority fees, landing services, MEV)

### Takeaway
For $1 trades, fixed per-transaction costs dominate. A 0.001 SOL landing tip is about $0.074, or 7.4% of the position per side, at SOL $73.66. So the cheapest viable path is Jupiter Ultra or Swap v2 `/order`+`/execute` (keyless today, 10 bps observed, Jupiter handles landing), or a self-built pump.fun/PumpSwap instruction sent through a low-tip path (Helius Sender SWQOS-only at 0.000005 SOL or a modest priority fee). PumpPortal Local adds 0.5% per trade and Lightning about 1%. The legacy Jupiter v6 hosts are retired. The current target is `api.jup.ag` with a free key, using Swap API v2 (launched Mar 2026).

### Inferences
- Per-side cost model for a $1 buy at SOL $73.66 (0.01358 SOL):
  - Base fee 5,000 lamports is about $0.0004.
  - Priority fee of 100k CU × 10,000 µlamports is about $0.00007.
  - A landing tip of 0.001 SOL is about $0.074, or 7.4% per side and about 15% round trip.
  - pump.fun curve fee is 1.25% per side.
  - PumpPortal adds 0.5–1%.
  - Jupiter Ultra charges 0.1%.
  - The token account needs about $0.11 of rent (refundable).

  A take-profit below roughly +20% can be eaten by tips and fees. The bot should avoid 0.001 SOL tip services and use Jupiter's managed `/execute` (fees bundled), Helius Sender SWQOS-only, or plain RPC sends with a small priority fee.
- The observed Ultra quote showed `outUsdValue` 0.933 versus `inUsdValue` 0.994, about a 6% gap, on a freshly graduated token. Slippage and impact on micro-caps can exceed fees, so quote-time checks are needed.
- Jupiter routes pump.fun AMM (PumpSwap) pools after graduation. Bonding-curve coins may need direct pump-program instructions or PumpPortal, which this session did not test.

### Gaps

- Official Jito block-engine rate limits were not found; only Helius's proxy limit was.
- NextBlock's official pricing and tip floor and bloXroute Solana dollar pricing were not found.
- The current pump.fun buy and sell discriminators and full account order were not verified; take them from the latest IDL.
